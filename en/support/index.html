Ecofin is free, open-source, and built for practical financial literacy. Support is welcome, but sponsors cannot influence articles, conclusions, recommendations, or methodology.

Trust and policy

Sponsorship firewall

Ecofin may criticize banks, consumer credit, trading, and interest-based lending. Any sponsorship is therefore strictly separated from editorial work.

Legal status

Early-stage company

Ecofin is an open-source financial education company. Public legal status, official support-service links, and payment details will be added here when they are ready.

Editorial firewall

Sponsors do not control content

  • Sponsors cannot edit, approve, or suppress articles.
  • Sponsored pages are clearly labeled.
  • The annual sponsor list is public.
  • Sponsored educational tools disclose their methodology.

Boundaries

No affiliate or harmful categories

Ecofin does not use affiliate links to loans, deposits, betting, trading, or speculative products. Sponsor categories that conflict with Islamic finance ethics are not accepted.

What support funds

  • New articles and translations
  • Calculators, charts, and practical tools
  • Hosting, domains, and maintenance
  • Open-source development

Responsible revenue

How Ecofin can stay free

The early goal is to build education-first revenue that is not tied to selling loans or speculative financial products.

Education and tools

  • Workshops and trainings for universities, student clubs, companies, and NGOs
  • White-label calculators and widgets for educational institutions, not loan-selling pages
  • B2B financial literacy curriculum for HR departments and universities

Content and grants

  • Sponsored public-interest series with editorial independence
  • Translation and localization services for financial education content
  • Grant-style institutional contracts while remaining for-profit

Transparency

Short public notes

Once support activity is established, Ecofin aims to publish short transparency notes at least quarterly. The annual sponsor list will be public, and sponsored educational tools will disclose their methodology.

Core rule

Sponsorship helps fund Ecofin's publishing, tools, hosting, and open-source work. Sponsors do not influence editorial topics, conclusions, recommendations, or criticism.

Temporary fallback

Direct transfer

Until an official support service is ready, direct transfer remains a temporary path for individual supporters. The policy on this page applies to this fallback too.
